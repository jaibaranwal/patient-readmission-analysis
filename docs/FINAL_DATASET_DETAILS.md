## Cleaned analytical file

The LMS file `patient_readmission_cleaned.csv` has 362 rows and 17 columns. Each row represents one completed inpatient encounter with a valid discharge date.

Important fields:
- `encounter_id`, `patient_id`, `hospital_id`: encounter and dimension keys.
- `start_date`, `discharge_date`: admission and discharge dates.
- `length_of_stay_days`: calendar-day difference between discharge and start.
- `next_inpatient_encounter_id`, `days_to_readmission`: next inpatient stay and day gap.
- `readmission_30d`: 1 when the next inpatient stay starts strictly after discharge and within 30 calendar days; otherwise 0.
- `age_at_admission`, `age_band`, `region`, `reason_category`: descriptive group fields.
- `total_cost`: source cost with invalid/negative values handled by the cleaning rules.

## Quality audit

Three encounter rows were excluded:
1. One row missing a required encounter/patient identifier.
2. One duplicate encounter identifier.
3. One encounter with a discharge date earlier than its start date.

One encounter with no discharge date was retained in the cleaned encounter file but excluded from the readmission denominator. One missing hospital reference was mapped to the explicit UNKNOWN hospital dimension member.

The cleaned fact file has unique encounter identifiers, no missing required fields and no SQLite foreign-key violations. Seven automated checks and 20 separate patient-level readmission comparisons passed.

## Interpretation

These records are synthetic. They are not patient records from an actual hospital and are not the official Synthea sample. The measures demonstrate the cleaning and readmission logic; they should not be used for clinical decisions or claims about hospital performance.
