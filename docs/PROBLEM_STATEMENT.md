# Problem statement

Hospitals need consistent summaries of inpatient encounters followed by another inpatient stay shortly after discharge. This project applies a clear 30-day, same-patient inpatient-to-inpatient definition to synthetic encounter records and compares descriptive patterns by month, hospital region, age band and encounter reason. The output is a reproducible Python/SQL workflow and dashboard, not a clinical risk score or an assessment of preventable readmissions.
